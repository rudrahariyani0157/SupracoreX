Frequently Asked

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Find quick resolutions to common development, maintenance, and project setup questions.

How long does a website take to build?

Typical Starter Landing pages take around 1 to 2 weeks. Custom booking systems for doctors, clinics, hotels, or large E-commerce portals take roughly 3 to 6 weeks, depending on complex integrations.

Do you offer website maintenance and updates?

Yes. We provide flexible maintenance retainer plans covering database backups, copy edits, security audits, and layout modifications to keep your platform running continuously.

Will my website be SEO-optimized?

Absolutely. Every layout we build undergoes clean HTML structural validations, Google PageSpeed speed optimizations, responsive audits, and schema markup deployments.

Can I edit the website content myself?

Yes. Business and Premium pricing tiers integrate a user-friendly Content Management System (CMS) panel allowing you to upload photos, update service packages, write blogs, or edit text options dynamically.

What is your typical development process?

Our workflow involves four main phases: Discovery & Architecture planning, Custom UI/UX Design, Hand-coded Development, and comprehensive Quality Assurance testing prior to deployment.
